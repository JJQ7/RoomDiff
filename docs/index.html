RoomDiff: what changed in the room?

Two phone videos of the same room, recorded at different times, compared in one shared 3D frame: objects reported as added, removed or moved, each with its evidence. Code, reports and benchmark numbers are in the GitHub repository.

Live viewer: SceneDiff benchmark pairs

  • living_room_1: bag and wallet removed, hairbrush added, ladder shelf and a curtain moved
  • office_1: one chair removed, one chair moved
  • bedroom_1: jacket removed, bedding and tissue box moved

Live viewer: our own tabletop recordings

  • tabletop_1: biscuit box added, tea canister removed, white box moved
  • tabletop_2: tea canister added, scissors removed, bucket moved
  • tabletop_3: detergent bottle moved, tea canister and stool hidden under the table edge

In the viewer: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, N / P for the next / previous change, R to reset the view. Red = removed, green = added, blue = moved (arrow from the old place), dimmed = unverified.
